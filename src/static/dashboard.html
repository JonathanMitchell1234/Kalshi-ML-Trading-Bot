How it works (methodology)

Direction probability = seed-averaged LightGBM, isotonic-calibrated on purged holdout folds (15m technicals, 1m microstructure, cross-asset leads, Hyperliquid funding, Coinbase premium, taker CVD, PCA factors; Bybit spread on BTC only). Trades only at |P−.5| ≥ 0.10 with fee-aware edge ≥ 0.03 (YES if P≥.6, NO if P≤.4), within 5 min of window open. Kalshi settles hourly binaries on CF BRTI; spot is Binance.US. Paper trades settle on the official result, net of taker fees.
